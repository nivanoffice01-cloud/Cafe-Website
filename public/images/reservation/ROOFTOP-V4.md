# Rooftop material assets — v4

These six photographic-style decorative illustrations are confined to the rooftop SVG. They are not claimed to be photographs of Mangosteen furniture or an exact floor survey.

- `rooftop-stone-v4.webp`: fine grey irregular flagstone material.
- `rooftop-table-long-v4.webp`, `rooftop-table-square-v4.webp`: walnut tabletop cutouts with subtle edge detail.
- `rooftop-chair-v4.webp`: consistent cream-cushion cane chair, one per existing seat ID.
- `rooftop-planter-trough-v4.webp`, `rooftop-planter-square-v4.webp`: architectural grey planters with foliage.

Generated with the built-in image tool, alpha retained where applicable, and encoded as optimized WebP. PNG originals remain in the image-generation library. Previous versions, indoor assets and genuine café photographs are preserved.

Source audit, exact source paths/dimensions/hashes, before/after previews and preservation tests: `artifacts/rooftop-map-v4/`.
