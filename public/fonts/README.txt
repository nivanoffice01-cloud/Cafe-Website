Manrope price subsets

Local 500/700 subsets from Google Fonts, containing U+20B9 (Indian rupee), digits, comma and period. The rupee and digits were verified in each font cmap. OFL licence retained in Manrope-OFL.txt.
Source stylesheet: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Manrope:wght@200..800&text=%E2%82%B90123456789%2C.&display=swap

Scoped to money displays; other typography is unchanged.
