# Source and licence record

The Blender study, geometry, procedural materials, Python script and qualification renders were authored for this project. No third-party model, photograph, image-generation output or texture is included in the study. It is an illustrative food concept, not a photograph or scan of an actual Mangosteen dish.

Sources examined, but **not incorporated**:

- Innerscene Whole pizza: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/whole-pizza-f7d99fd1 — CC0 declaration on the source page and in the downloaded GLB. Temporarily inspected: 37 meshes, 9,960 triangles, seven base-colour textures, no normal/roughness texture channels. Actual local render rejected for faceted toppings, a smooth tubular rim and flat cheese. The temporary GLB is `/private/tmp/mangosteen-pizza-candidate.glb`; it is not a website asset.
- BlendSwap Pizza vegetarian: https://blendswap.com/blend/31290 — listed CC0, download requires sign-in. Public preview inspected; ingredient separation and complete surfaces unverified. Not acquired.
- Blendkit Pizza scan: https://www.blendkit.com/asset-gallery-detail/35f655f4-c568-4c13-bd85-e49547dd38f2/ — listed free, royalty-free whole-pizza scan. Separate ingredient meshes and suitability of public WebGL distribution unverified. Not acquired.
- Fab Pizza Pack: https://www.fab.com/listings/ce6ebbf1-94f4-4f5c-b3c8-71fa9acf0e7f — advertised photogrammetry/2K PBR assets. Separate ingredient meshes not established. No purchase made.
- BlendSwap Low-Poly Pizza: https://blendswap.com/blend/21782 — CC0, separate crust/toppings described, but explicitly low-poly. Rejected for this visual brief.

Official Blender installer source: https://download.blender.org/release/Blender4.5/blender-4.5.9-macos-arm64.dmg . No third-party installer or rendering service was used.
